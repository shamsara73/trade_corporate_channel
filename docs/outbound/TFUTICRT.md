## Points to check with the TI team

* **Same tag, same type, different field name:** `CurrencySend` and `TnxCurrencySend` share `AMT-S,c`; `AmountSend` and `TnxAmountSend` share `AMT-S,u`; `Currency` and `TnxCurrency` share `AMT-U,c`; `Amount` and `TnxAmount` share `AMT-U,u`; `OutstandingCcy` and `AvailableCcy` share `OUA,c`; `OutstandingAmount` and `AvailableAmt` share `OUA,u`. If these are meant to differ (for example `Amount` vs `TnxAmount`, `OutstandingAmount` vs `AvailableAmt`), the mapping gives them identical values.
* **Same field name twice:** `AttachedDocuments`, `Charges` (two `Charges` tables with tags `AAOC` and `AABC`, two `AttachedDocuments` tables with tags `GDOCS` and `tAATT`). A consumer must be able to tell them apart by tag.
* **Tag reused across types:** `DAD` is both a flag and a day count, and `DFRP` both an integer and a string. Treat tag **and** type as the key.
* **25 fields have only a low-confidence counterpart** (the renewal and reduction groups need a business check): `InstructingPartyRef`, `OurRequestTypeCodeSend`, `NotesToIssuingBank`, `PrincipalID`, `AddAmountAndDetailsSend`, `RenewalSend`, `RenewalBasisTypeSend`, `RegularRenewOnTypeSend`, `RenewalOnExpirySend`, `RenewalOnCalendarDateSend`, `RenewalCalendarDaysSend`, `FinalExpiryDateSend`, `MaximumDecreaseSend`, `RedIncByAmountSend`, `AddAmountAndDetails`, `Renewal`, `RenewalBasisType`, `RegularRenewOnType`, `RenewalOnExpiry`, `RenewalOnCalendarDate`, `RenewalCalendarDays`, `FinalExpiryDate`, `MaximumDecrease`, `RedIncByAmount`, `DeliveryOfUndertakingOther`.
